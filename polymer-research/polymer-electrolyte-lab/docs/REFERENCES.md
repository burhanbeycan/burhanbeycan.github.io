# Primary sources and definitions

1. France-Lanord, A.; Grossman, J. C. **Correlations from Ion Pairing and the Nernst–Einstein Equation.** Physical Review Letters 122, 136001 (2019). https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.122.136001 — Why ideal self-diffusion estimates and correlated charge transport differ. The cluster approximation from this paper is not implemented here.
2. **Transference Number in Polymer Electrolytes: Mind the Reference-Frame Gap.** Journal of the American Chemical Society (2022). https://doi.org/10.1021/jacs.2c02389 ; open text https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9074101/ — Frame-dependent transport coefficients and transference numbers. This app explicitly defines its flux normalization; coefficients from other conventions must be converted by the researcher before entry.
3. NIST. **CODATA recommended values of the fundamental physical constants: 2022.** https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/pdf/wall_2022.pdf — SI defining constants, derived molar gas and Faraday constants.

No literature data have been fitted or reproduced as experiments in this release. Original numerical implementation; citations identify the scientific models, not borrowed code.
