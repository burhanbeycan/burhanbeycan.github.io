# References and verification sources

1. Meaurio, E. **Generalization of the Carothers equation for linear step growth polymers.** *Scientific Reports* **14**, 27281 (2024). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-78476-2). [Open full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11549450/). Used for the functional-group/molecule balance, scope of acyclic assumptions and the independently checked 99 mol AA/100 mol BB full-conversion Xn=199 reference case. The app implements only the basic bifunctional AA+BB case, not the paper's generalized chain-stopper treatment.
2. IUPAC Gold Book. [Chain polymerization](https://old.goldbook.iupac.org/html/C/C00958.html). Terminology of propagation at regenerated reactive sites. The software's initiated-chain mass balance follows directly from its stated conservation assumptions; this definition is not evidence of actual living behavior for any named polymer.
3. IUPAC Gold Book. [Copolymerisation composition equation](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/15391). Definition and instantaneous-composition scope of the Mayo–Lewis equation.
4. **IUPAC recommended experimental methods and data evaluation procedures for the determination of radical copolymerization reactivity ratios from composition data.** *Polymer Chemistry* **15**, 1851–1861 (2024). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1039/D4PY00270A). Reactivity-ratio definitions, terminal assumptions and distinction between low-conversion instantaneous fitting and integrated composition analysis.

Equations and conservation identities are independently implemented and documented; no tables, figures or text from the sources are copied. Source 1 full text was retrieved on 23 November 2026. The IUPAC definitions and source 4 were checked through retrieved bibliographic/definition and equation search records; publisher full-text access to source 4 was unavailable in this environment, so no full-text review of that source is claimed.
